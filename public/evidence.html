when it matters.

Leadership is reflected in the ability of a team to coordinate, adapt and provide a reliable service under difficult circumstances.

External correspondence · private original

Ontario Provincial Police — recognition of assisting services

February 2016 · Sault Ste. Marie Detachment

Following a challenging remote winter emergency in February 2016, the OPP expressed thanks to Searchmont Fire Department, Search and Rescue and the other responding personnel. The correspondence identifies me as Fire Chief among the personnel involved.

Context

Remote terrain, severe weather, limited communications and resource constraints created demanding conditions for a multi-agency response.

What the correspondence recognizes

The acting detachment commander praised the collective commitment, teamwork and coordinated deployment of personnel and equipment. A responding OPP sergeant also recognized the dedication and value of the volunteer service.

Application to research leadership

Maintain clear roles, build trusted partnerships, allocate resources deliberately and support specialists in delivering a shared outcome, even when information is incomplete.

Evidence boundary: This is a summary of correspondence supplied by me, not a formal personal award or a claim that I alone delivered the response. The original email is retained privately; no incident identifiers, third-party contact information or original message are published here.
