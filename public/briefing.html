Proposed research leadership agenda

  1. One shared customer understanding. Agree on common research questions, definitions and appropriate linkage across customer feedback, surveys, behavioural and market evidence. Keep evidence provenance visible.
  2. Research excellence as a standard. Build consistent instruments, sampling plans, weighting, tabulations and uncertainty disclosure. Peer-review qualitative coding and analytical models.
  3. Investment tied to decisions. Prioritize the research portfolio around evidence gaps, decisions, accessibility and player health. Manage capacity, procurement and overlap explicitly.
  4. People and partnerships first. Develop specialists through coaching, stretch assignments and constructive review; build shared priorities with enterprise and cross-jurisdictional partners.
  5. Measure value after the decision. Track decision adoption, research usefulness, delivery and quality. Agree customer outcomes and appropriate evaluation designs before claiming impact.

First 90 days

Days 1–30 · Listen & diagnose

  • Meet the team and key stakeholders
  • Review existing research, suppliers and budget
  • Understand methodology and quality risks
  • Agree priority decisions and customer outcomes

Days 31–60 · Align & design

  • Agree common tracking and evidence standards
  • Align the research agenda and capacity
  • Plan a human-led Voice of Customer pilot
  • Co-create individual development goals

Days 61–90 · Deliver & learn

  • Launch agreed priority research
  • Publish an executive insights briefing
  • Agree cross-jurisdiction research opportunities
  • Evaluate decisions and refine the next quarter

How I would measure progress

Agree appropriate indicators for research quality, delivery, adoption, usefulness and coaching. Partner with decision owners to define service outcomes, accessibility and player-health measures and assess impact through suitable evaluations.

Research integrity

Document evidence provenance, limitations, review, privacy and appropriate use. Preserve research independence. Require external validation before operational model use. Do not use inferred vulnerability for promotion.
